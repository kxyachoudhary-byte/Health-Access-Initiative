03 / research methods

Reproducible by design.

Clear provenance and stated limitations matter more than a dramatic map.

01 · Town selection

Official Census incorporated places classified as “town.” For each of four states, ten locations sampled at equal intervals from a GEOID-sorted list. Selected towns are not asserted to be the most underserved.

02 · Geographic distance

Great-circle (haversine) miles between each town’s Census representative point and HRSA service-delivery-site coordinates. Includes nearest and third-nearest centers, rather than road-network driving distances.

03 · Exploratory clustering

Two standardized distance features, grouped with deterministic Lloyd K-means (k=3). Group names mean closer/intermediate/farther within these 40 towns only, not medically validated shortage categories.

Model limitations. Straight-line distances are based on Census town representative points and HRSA-listed service-delivery sites, including some school-based, mobile, and other special-purpose records. Distances are not road travel times, and the clusters are exploratory proximity groups rather than official shortage designations. This code exports standard QGIS-compatible GeoJSON.

Primary sources

U.S. Census Bureau, places Gazetteer · HRSA Health Center Program · HRSA GIS service-delivery-site layer. The downloaded full JSON contains the exact source endpoints and query parameters.
