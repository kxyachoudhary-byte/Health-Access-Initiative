# Exploratory Karnataka access screening

**Geocoded villages examined:** 5,109 of 30,753 village directory records (16.6% coverage).

**Mapped PHC/CHC records within geographic vicinity:** 4,148.

**Candidate villages ≥10 km (straight-line) from a mapped PHC/CHC:** 85.

**K-Means planning clusters:** 40 (requested 40, a modeling choice).

These figures are an exploratory geographic screening result, **not confirmation of any number of healthcare deserts**. See run_metadata.json for sources, SHA-256 hashes, geographic scope, and coverage limitations. Verify candidate sites against road travel time, facility operations, capacity, additional datasets, and community input before citing them as shortage areas.
