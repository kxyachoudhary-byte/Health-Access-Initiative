# Potential external data sources

These sources are offered for independent collection and evaluation. They have **not** been processed to establish any findings in this repository.

- Indian healthcare facility locations: https://github.com/yashveeeeeeer/india-geodata/blob/main/data/healthcare/facilities/README.md
- National Informatics Centre HealthGIS: https://healthgis.in/
- DataMeet Indian village boundaries: https://projects.datameet.org/indian_village_boundaries/
- Census of India Population Finder (2011): https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/en/data/population-finder
- Government Rural Health Statistics: https://main.mohfw.gov.in/documents/statistics/rural-health-statistics

Verify original collection period, coverage, licensing, facility status, geometry quality and join keys. Data absent from a directory is not proof that a village has no healthcare access. Document each source's version, retrieval date, transformations and license before publication.
